Why Use LeashFree.ca’s Dog Directory

LeashFree.ca was built to bring Canadian dog owners and trusted service providers together in one reliable place.
When you use our directory, you’ll find:

  • Verified Listings – We manually review each organization before publishing.
  • Easy Search by City or Category – Explore dog services anywhere in Canada.
  • Map-Ready Addresses – Every listing includes a complete address for accurate location results.
  • Community Focus – From local rescues to small businesses, we feature organizations that put dogs first.
  • No Paywalls for Nonprofits – Shelters and rescues are always listed for free to help dogs find homes.

How to Get Featured

If you represent a Canadian dog-related business, organization, or nonprofit, we’d love to include you. You can submit your listing or claim an existing one by contacting our team. We’re always expanding our database to support Canada’s dog-loving community.
