## How To Use This Tracker

Update this file whenever an article changes state, research changes the priority, or a published post creates a follow-up opportunity.

Statuses:

- `Proposed`: Next recommended item, awaiting confirmation or research kickoff.
- `Backlog`: Ranked but not started.
- `In Research`: Sources, outline, and claims are being validated.
- `Ready To Draft`: Brief is complete and source set is strong enough.
- `Drafting`: Article is being written or implemented.
- `Review`: Needs editorial, factual, SEO, or internal-link review.
- `Ready To Publish`: Approved and waiting for deployment.
- `Published`: Live on LeashFree.ca.
- `Parked`: Kept for later because timing, business fit, or evidence is not strong enough yet.

## Update Rules

- Re-rank monthly, or sooner if a new tool, sponsor, affiliate opportunity, or seasonal event changes business value.
- Every article must have a clear internal destination before drafting.
- Every article must list at least 3 high-trust external sources before moving to `Ready To Draft`.
- Every health or safety article must include conservative veterinary guidance and "when to call a vet."
- Every cost, insurance, food, or gear article must include examples, assumptions, and caveats.
- Every published post should be linked from at least 2 existing LeashFree pages within the same cluster.

## Article Completion Checklist

Use this checklist before marking an article `Ready To Publish`.

- [ ] Search intent and reader promise are explicit.
- [ ] At least 3 high-trust external sources are cited.
- [ ] At least 5 internal links are included.
- [ ] Primary destination/tool/directory CTA is present.
- [ ] Article has a quick answer or practical summary near the top.
- [ ] Article includes a table, checklist, calculator example, or decision matrix where useful.
- [ ] Health/safety claims are conservative and include vet guidance.
- [ ] Cost/insurance/food claims include assumptions and caveats.
- [ ] Meta title and meta description are drafted.
- [ ] FAQ section covers common follow-up searches.
- [ ] Existing related pages are updated to link to the new post.
- [ ] Final URL is added to this tracker after publication.
