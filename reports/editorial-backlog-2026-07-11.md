## First Article Brief

### Title

Pet Insurance in Canada: What Dog Owners Should Compare Before They Buy

### Search Intent

Canadian dog owners comparing whether pet insurance is worth it, which policy terms matter, and how to avoid buying coverage that does not match their dog's actual risk.

### Reader Promise

By the end, the reader should know how to compare policies without being misled by the monthly premium alone.

### Required Sections

1. Quick answer: what matters most when comparing dog insurance in Canada.
2. How pet insurance works: reimbursement, deductible, co-insurance, annual limits, exclusions, and claims.
3. The 10 policy details to compare before buying.
4. Accident-only vs accident-and-illness vs wellness add-ons.
5. Waiting periods and pre-existing conditions.
6. Breed, age, location, and renewal increases.
7. Annual limits: unlimited vs $10,000 vs lower limits.
8. Emergency vet bill scenarios.
9. Self-insuring vs buying insurance.
10. Questions to ask before signing up.
11. Internal tool workflow: estimate costs, compare annual limits, find a vet, and use the coming Dog Insurance Finder.
12. FAQ.

### Internal Links To Include

- `/resources/dog-cost-calculator-canada/`
- `/blog/is-dog-insurance-worth-it-canada/`
- `/blog/dog-insurance-waiting-periods-canada/`
- `/blog/unlimited-vs-10000-annual-limit/`
- `/blog/emergency-vet-costs-canada/`
- `/blog/average-vet-costs-in-canada/`
- `/directory/veterinarians/`
- `/resources/`

### Source Requirements

Use primary or high-trust sources where possible:

- NAPHIA State of the Industry materials for insured pets, premiums, and industry growth.
- Canadian insurance regulator/consumer guidance where relevant.
- Provider policy documents only for examples, not broad claims.
- Veterinary sources for emergency-care and breed-health context.
- LeashFree's own calculator assumptions for internal examples.

### Quality Bar

This article should be built like a buying worksheet, not a fluffy explainer. It should include:

- A comparison table of policy features.
- A "monthly premium is not enough" example.
- A sample emergency bill scenario.
- A checklist the reader can use while shopping.
- Clear disclaimers that LeashFree is not an insurer, broker, veterinarian, or financial advisor.
